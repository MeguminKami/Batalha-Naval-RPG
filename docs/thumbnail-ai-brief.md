# Thumbnail AI Brief: Batalha Naval RPG

Use this document to brief an image creator AI for a promotional thumbnail based on the existing project icon:

Reference image: `assets/images/gameicon.png`

## Project Context

`Batalha Naval RPG` is a fantasy battleship game built around naval combat, tactical grid attacks, character classes, skills, ship placement, and turn-based combat. The game mixes classic Battleship mechanics with RPG-style heroes and abilities.

The visual identity is not realistic military simulation. It should feel like a polished fantasy game logo: heroic, ornate, dramatic, and readable.

## Reference Image Description

The existing `gameicon.png` shows:

- A centered fantasy crest/emblem.
- A large dark navy shield or plaque as the main background.
- Ornamental gold and bronze framing.
- A golden crown or helmet shape at the top.
- Wing-like carved golden elements around the sides.
- Large 3D gold serif lettering: `BATALHA`.
- Smaller centered gold text below: `NAVAL`.
- Smallest centered gold text below that: `RPG`.
- High contrast between warm metallic gold and deep navy blue.
- A clean, premium game-logo look with painterly fantasy rendering.

## Thumbnail Goal

Create a bold thumbnail that immediately communicates:

- This is the same `Batalha Naval RPG` game.
- It is about tactical naval combat.
- It has RPG/fantasy energy, not a plain board-game look.
- It is exciting enough for a YouTube/video/store preview thumbnail.

## Main Creative Direction

Use `gameicon.png` as the brand anchor. Keep the same core identity:

- Gold 3D fantasy lettering.
- Deep navy shield/crest.
- Crown/helmet motif.
- Ornamental gold frame.
- Dramatic highlights and shadows.

Expand the thumbnail around it with action context:

- Ocean or stormy sea background.
- Tactical grid hints, like glowing square tiles or targeting markers.
- Ships, cannons, explosions, missiles, or magical/energy attacks.
- A sense of turn-based battle strategy.
- Optional split mood: pirate/captain/space commander factions suggested through subtle elements.

## Composition

Preferred composition for a 16:9 thumbnail:

- Place the logo/crest slightly left of center or centered.
- Keep `BATALHA NAVAL RPG` large and readable.
- Add action behind or around the logo, not over the text.
- Use a dramatic diagonal flow: ships/attacks from lower left toward upper right.
- Keep the logo as the first thing the viewer notices.
- Leave enough contrast around the text so it is readable at small sizes.

For a square thumbnail:

- Center the crest.
- Add action effects around the edges.
- Keep the text readable and avoid cropping the crown or bottom point of the emblem.

## Color and Lighting

Use this palette:

- Metallic gold: primary logo and highlights.
- Deep navy blue: shield, UI/game identity, shadows.
- Warm bronze: ornamental frame and carved details.
- White/yellow highlights: explosions, glints, magic effects.
- Blue/cyan accents: tactical grid, ocean glow, space commander energy.
- Red/orange accents: cannon fire and impact effects.

Lighting should be high contrast:

- Bright rim lights on the gold.
- Strong shadows under 3D text.
- Subtle bloom/glow on explosions and targeting effects.

## Typography

Text should match the existing fantasy game-logo style:

- Large golden serif/fantasy letters.
- 3D beveled edges.
- Dark shadow under letters.
- High readability.

Required text:

```text
BATALHA
NAVAL
RPG
```

Do not misspell the title. Do not replace it with English words.

## Prompt To Use

```text
Create a high-impact fantasy game thumbnail for "BATALHA NAVAL RPG", based on the provided reference logo image. Preserve the identity of the reference: a dark navy shield crest, ornate metallic gold frame, golden crown or helmet at the top, and large beveled 3D gold fantasy lettering reading "BATALHA NAVAL RPG". Expand the scene into a dramatic tactical naval battle: stormy ocean, grid-based strategy hints, glowing targeting squares, ship silhouettes, cannon fire, explosions, and subtle RPG magic/energy effects. The logo must remain the hero of the image and the title text must be perfectly readable at small thumbnail size. Use premium fantasy game art styling, cinematic lighting, high contrast gold and navy palette, polished painterly rendering, sharp edges on the logo, and dynamic action around the emblem without covering the words. Output a clean 16:9 promotional thumbnail.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
blurry text, misspelled title, unreadable logo, low contrast, flat icon, plain white background, realistic modern military UI, cartoon doodle style, excessive clutter over the title, cropped text, cropped crown, random extra words, different game name, dull colors, muddy lighting, cheap mobile ad style, distorted letters, broken typography
```

## Output Requirements

- Primary output: 16:9 thumbnail, ideally `1920x1080`.
- Optional square output: `1024x1024`.
- Keep the title readable when scaled down to 320px wide.
- Export as PNG.
- Do not remove the fantasy crest identity from the reference.
- Do not hide the logo behind ships, explosions, or effects.
